# Launch Disclosures

## MLB Marks And Content
- MLB club names, logos, marks, and related media remain the property of MLB and the respective clubs
- the product should attribute those rights clearly and avoid republishing licensed editorial body text without permission

## User Content
- commenting requires verified authentication
- comments are plain text only
- links, images, and abusive content are restricted
- moderation actions and abuse strikes are logged for review

## AI Use
- AI answers are limited to baseball and AiBS analytics contexts
- misuse attempts can result in AI timeouts or bans
- AI requests and safety events are logged for abuse review and cost control

## Privacy
- minimize stored PII
- keep private user routes uncached in shared caches
- support AI-history purge and account deletion paths
